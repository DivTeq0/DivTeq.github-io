    Section 9.4 Problems

    Checkpoint 9.4.1.

    Find \(w'(t)\) at \(t=1\) if
    \begin{equation*} w=xe^y+y\sin z-\cos z\qquad x=e^t\quad y=t-1+\ln t\quad z=\pi t^2 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 1.
    At \(t=1\)
    \begin{equation*} x=e \quad y=0 \quad z=\pi \quad\Rightarrow\quad w=e+1 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x'=e^t\quad y.=1+1/t\quad z'=2\pi t \quad\Rightarrow\quad x'(1)=e\quad y'(1)=2 \quad z'(1)=2\pi \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} w_x=e^y \qquad w_y=xe^y+\sin z \qquad w_z=y\cos z+\sin z \end{equation*}
    \begin{align*} w'(1) \amp = w_xx'(1)+w_yy'(1)+w_zz'(1)\\ \amp =(1)e+(e)2+(0)(2\pi)=3e \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 9.4.2.

    Find \(\partial_uw\partial_vw\) if
    \begin{equation*} w=ze^{x+2y}-2xz+zy^2 \qquad x=2u-v^2\quad y=u+1 \quad z=uv \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 2.
    \begin{equation*} w_x=ze^{x+2y}-2z\qquad w_y=2ze^{x+2y}+2zy\qquad w_z=e^{x+2y}+y^2 \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} x_u=2 \quad x_v=-2v \qquad y_u=1 \quad y_v=0 \quad z_u=v\quad z_v=u \end{equation*}
    \begin{align*} \partial_uw \amp =(ze^{x+2y}-2z)2+(2ze^{x+2y}+2zy)(1)+(e^{x+2y}+y^2)(v)\\ \amp =(uve^{2-v^2}-2uv)(2)+(uve^{2-v^2}+2uv(u+1))(1)\\ \amp +(e^{2-v^2}+(u+1)^2)(v) \end{align*}
    \begin{align*} \partial_vw \amp =(ze^{x+2y}-2z)(-2v)+(2ze^{x+2y}+2zy)(0)+(e^{x+2y}+y^2)(u)\\ \amp =(uve^{2-v^2}-2uv)(-2v)+(e^{2-v^2}+(u+1)^2)(u) \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 9.4.3.

    If
    \begin{equation*} w=x^2y^6\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right) \end{equation*}
    Calculate
    \begin{equation*} x\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial w}{\partial y} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 3.
    \begin{equation*} \frac{\partial w}{\partial x}=2xy^6\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)+x^2y^6\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-(y/x)^2}}\left(-\frac{y}{x^2}\right)\right) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} \frac{\partial w}{\partial x}=6x^2y^5\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)+x^2y^6\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-(y/x)^2}}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)\right) \end{equation*}
    \begin{align*} x\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}+y\frac{\partial w}{\partial y} \amp =2x^2y^6\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)+6x^2y^6\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)\\ \amp +\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-(y/x)^2}}\left(x^3y^6\left(-\frac{y}{x^2}\right)\right)+\left(x^2y^7\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)\right)\\ \amp = 8x^2y^6\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)=8w \end{align*}
    which could have been deduced using Euler’s theorem since \(w\) is homogeneous of order 8.
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 9.4.4.

    Given
    \begin{equation*} w=\frac{\ln(xy^3)}{x^2+y^2} \end{equation*}
    find \(\partial_r w,\partial_\theta w\) at the point \(r=2,\theta=\pi/4\)
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 4.
    \begin{equation*} w_x=\frac{1}{x(x^2+y^2)}+\ln(xy^3)\left(\frac{-2x}{(x^2+y^2)^2}\right) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} w_y=\frac{3}{y(x^2+y^2)}+\ln(xy^3)\left(\frac{-2y}{(x^2+y^2)^2}\right) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} \partial_rx=\cos\theta \quad \partial_ry=\sin\theta \quad \partial_\theta x=-r\sin\theta \quad \partial_\theta y=r\cos\theta \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} w_r=w_x\,x_r+w_y\,y_r \qquad w_\theta=w_x\,x_\theta+w_y\,y_\theta \end{equation*}
    At the point \(x=y=\sqrt{2}\)
    \begin{equation*} \partial_rx=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \quad \partial_ry=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \quad \partial_\theta x=-\sqrt{2} \quad \partial_\theta y=\sqrt{2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} w_x=\frac{1}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right) \quad w_y=\frac{3}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right) \end{equation*}
    \begin{align*} \partial_rw \amp =\left(\frac{1}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right)\right)\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp +\left(\frac{3}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right) \right)\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp =\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\ln 4}{8} \end{align*}
    \begin{align*} \partial_\theta w \amp =\left(\frac{1}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right)\right)(-\sqrt{2})\\ \amp +\left(\frac{3}{4\sqrt{2}}+\ln(4)\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{16}\right) \right)\sqrt{2}\\ \amp =\frac{1}{2} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 9.4.5.

    Find \(z_x,z_y\) at the point \((-3,2,1)\) on the surfacw
    \begin{equation*} f(x,y,z)=xy^2+yz^3+xyz=-16 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 5.
    \begin{equation*} f_x=y^2+yz\qquad f_y=2xy+z^3+xz\qquad f_z=3yz^2+xy \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} f_x(-3,2,1)=6 \qquad f_y(-3,2,1)=-14 \qquad f_z(-3,2,1)=0 \end{equation*}
    So \(z_x.z_y\) don’t exist.
