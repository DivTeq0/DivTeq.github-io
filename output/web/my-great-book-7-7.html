    Section 5.6 Problems

    Checkpoint 5.6.1.

    (a) Find the length of the curve
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}=\langle 2t^2+1, \frac{2t^3}{3},1-t^2\rangle \qquad 0\leq t\leq 2 \end{equation*}
    (b) Find the equation of the tangent line to the curve at the point\((3,2/3,0)\)
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 1.
    (a)
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}'=\langle 4t,2t^2,-2t\rangle \quad |{\bf r'}|=2\sqrt{4t^2+t^4+t^2}=2t\sqrt{t^2+5} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} \mbox{Arc length}=\int_0^22t\sqrt{t^2+5}\,dt=\left[\frac{2}{3}(t^2+5)^{3/2}\right]_0^2=\frac{2}{3}(27-5^{3/2}) \end{equation*}
    (b) The point corresponds to \(t=1\) so the tangent line will be
    \begin{equation*} {\bf l}(u)={\bf r}(1)+u{\bf r}'(1)=\left\langle 3,\frac{2}{3},0\right\rangle +u\langle 4,2,-2\rangle \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.6.2.

    Find the unit tangent, unit normal, binormal, and curvature for the curve
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}=\langle 6t,3t^2,t^3\rangle \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 2.
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}'=\langle 6,6t,3t^2\rangle=3\langle 2,2t,t^2\rangle \qquad |{\bf r}'|=3(t^2+2) \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}''=\langle 0,6,6t\rangle \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}'\times{\bf r}''=18\left|\begin{array}{ccc} {\bf i}\amp {\bf j}\amp {\bf k} \\ 2 \amp 2t\amp t^2 \\ 0 \amp 1\amp t\end{array}\right|=18\langle t^2,-2t,2\rangle \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} |{\bf r}'\times{\bf r}''|=18(t^2+2)\quad\Rightarrow \quad \kappa=\frac{18(t^2+2)}{27(t^2+2)^3)}=\frac{2}{3(t^2+2)^2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} {\bf T}=\frac{\langle 2,2t,t^2\rangle}{t^2+2}\qquad {\bf B}=\frac{\langle t^2,-2t,2\rangle}{t^2+2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{align*} {\bf N} \amp ={\bf B}\times{\bf T}=\frac{2}{(t^2+2)^2}\left|\begin{array}{ccc} {\bf i}\amp {\bf j}\amp {\bf k} \\ t^2 \amp -2t\amp 2 \\ 2 \amp 2t\amp t^2\end{array}\right|\\ \amp =\frac{\langle -4t-2t^3,4-t^4,2t^3+4t\rangle}{(t^2+2)^2}=\frac{\langle -2t,2-t^2,2t\rangle}{t^2+2} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.6.3.

    Find the unit tangent, unit normal, binormal, and curvature of the curve
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}=\langle\cos t+t\sin t,\sin t-t\cos t,t^2\rangle \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 3.
    \begin{align*} {\bf r}' \amp = \langle t\cos t,t\sin t,2t\rangle \qquad |{\bf r}'|=\sqrt{5}t\\ {\bf T} \amp = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\langle \cos t,\sin t,2\rangle\\ {\bf T}' \amp =\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\langle -\sin t,\cos t,0\rangle \\ {\bf N} \amp =\frac{{\bf T}'}{|{\bf T}'|}=\langle -\sin t,\cos t,0\rangle\\ \kappa \amp = \frac{{\bf T}'}{|{\bf r}'|}=\frac{1}{5t} \end{align*}
    \begin{align*} {\bf B} \amp ={\bf T}\times{\bf N}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\left|\begin{array}{ccc} {\bf i}\amp {\bf j}\amp {\bf k} \\ \cos t \amp \sin t\amp 2 \\ -\sin t \amp \cos t\amp 0\end{array}\right|\\ \amp ==\frac{\langle -2\cos t,-2\sin t,1\rangle}{\sqrt{5}} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.6.4.

    (a) Show that the curve
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}=\langle 2t,\cos 2t,-\cos 2t\rangle \end{equation*}
    lies in a plane
    🔗
    (b) Find the equation of the plane
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 4.
    (a)
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}'=\langle 2,-2\sin 2t,2\sin 2t\rangle \qquad {\bf r}''=\langle 0,-4\cos 2t,4\cos 2t 2t\rangle \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}'\times{\bf r}''=\left|\begin{array}{ccc} {\bf i}\amp {\bf j}\amp {\bf k} \\ 2 \amp -2\sin 2t\amp 2\sin 2t \\ 0 \amp -4\cos 2t\amp 4\cos 2t\end{array}\right|=\langle 0, -8\cos 2t,-8\cos 2t\rangle \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} {\bf B}=\frac{{\bf r}'\times{\bf r}''}{|{\bf r}'\times{\bf r}''|}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\langle 0,-1,-1\rangle \end{equation*}
    The binormal is constant so the osculating plane is also
    🔗
    (b)
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}(0)=\langle 0,1,-1\rangle \end{equation*}
    So the equation of the plane is
    \begin{equation*} \langle 0,-1,-1\rangle\cdot\langle x,y-1,z+1\rangle =0\quad\Rightarrow\quad y+z=0 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.6.5.

    Parametrize the curve of intersection of the two surfaces
    \begin{equation*} x^2+y^2-2x+4y=4 \qquad z=x^2+y^3 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 5.
    The cylinder equation can be rewritten as
    \begin{equation*} (x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=9 \end{equation*}
    which can be parametrized as
    \begin{equation*} x=1+3\cos t\quad y=-2+3\sin t \qquad 0\leq t\leq 2\pi \end{equation*}
    Substituting into the equation for the paraboloid
    \begin{equation*} z=(3\cos t+1)^2+(3\sin t-2)^2=14+6\cos t-12\sin t \end{equation*}
    The curve can be parametrized as
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}=\langle 1+3\cos t,-2+3\sin t,14+6\cos t-12\sin t\rangle \quad o\leq t\leq 2\pi \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.6.6.

    Find the unit tangent, unit norma, binormal, and curvature of the curve
    \begin{equation*} {\bf r}\langle \ln(1+t^2),t-2\tan^{-1}t,t\rangle \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer 6.
    \begin{align*} {\bf r}'= \amp \left\langle \frac{2t}{1+t^2},1-\frac{2}{1+t^2},1\right\rangle=\frac{\langle 2t,t^2-1,t^2+1\rangle}{(1+t^2)}\\ |{\bf r}'|= \amp \frac{1}{(t^2+1)}\sqrt{4t^2+(t^2-1)^2+t^2+1)^2}=\sqrt{2}\amp \\ {\bf T} =\amp \frac{\langle 2t,t^2-1,t^2+1\rangle}{(1+t^2)}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left\langle \frac{2t}{1+t^2},1-\frac{2}{1+t^2},1\right\rangle\\ {\bf T}'= \amp \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left\langle \frac{2}{1+t^2}-\frac{4t}{(1+t^2)^2},\frac{4t^2}{(1+t^2)^2},0\right\rangle=\frac{\langle 2-2t^2,4t,0\rangle}{\sqrt{2}(1+t^2)^2}\\ {\bf N}= \amp \frac{{\bf T}'}{|{\bf T}'|}=\frac{\langle 1-t^2,2t,0\rangle}{1+t^2}\\ \kappa = \amp \frac{{\bf T}'}{|{\bf r}'|}=\frac{1}{1+t^2} \end{align*}
    \begin{align*} {\bf B} =\amp {\bf T}\times{\bf N}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}(1+t^2)^2}\left|\begin{array}{ccc} {\bf i}\amp {\bf j}\amp {\bf k} \\ 2t \amp t^2-1\amp t^2+1 \\ 1-t^2 \amp 2t\amp 0\end{array}\right|\\ = \amp \frac{\langle -2t(1+t^2),1-t^4,(1+t^2)^2\rangle}{\sqrt{2}(1+t^2)^2}=\frac{\langle -2t,1-t^2,1+t^2\rangle}{\sqrt{2}(1+t^2)} \end{align*}
